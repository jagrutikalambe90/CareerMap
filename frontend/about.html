About CareerMap

CareerMap is a web-based career exploration and guidance platform designed to help students understand different career opportunities and identify the skills required to achieve their career goals.

🎯 Our Purpose

CareerMap provides students with simple and organized career information in one place. It helps users explore careers, understand required qualifications and skills, take a career quiz, save interesting careers, and identify skills they may need to learn.

✨ What CareerMap Offers

🔎 Explore Careers

Search and explore different technology and computer-related career opportunities.

🧠 Career Quiz

Answer simple questions and receive career recommendations based on your interests.

💾 Save Careers

Save careers that interest you and view them later from your saved careers section.

🧩 Skill Gap Analyzer

Compare your existing skills with the skills required for a selected career.

💻 Technologies Used

Frontend: HTML, CSS and JavaScript

Backend: Python with Flask

Database & Authentication: Supabase

👩‍🎓 Who Can Use CareerMap?

CareerMap is designed mainly for students and beginners who want to explore computer science and technology-related career options and understand what they can learn to prepare for those careers.
